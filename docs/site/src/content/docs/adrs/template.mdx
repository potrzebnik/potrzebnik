Copy the block below to `docs/site/src/content/docs/adrs/NNNN-short-title.mdx`, take the next free
number, and fill in the four sections. Keep it to a page; if it needs more, it is probably two
decisions.

```mdx
---
title: 'NNNN: Short title naming the decision'
description: One sentence a reader can scan in a sidebar.
---

## Context

The forces in play: the requirement, constraint, or pain that made a choice necessary, and the
options realistically on the table. Present tense, factual, not yet arguing for the outcome.

## Decision

What was chosen, stated as a decision the project has taken - "The app uses X", not "we should
probably use X". Name the rejected alternatives and the constraint that ruled each out.

## Consequences

What is now true because of this, good and bad: what becomes easy, what becomes awkward, what
obligation the team has accepted, and what would have to happen to reverse it.

## Status

One of `Proposed`, `Accepted`, or `Deprecated`, with the date.
```
